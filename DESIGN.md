## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for high-velocity real estate dealmakers and agents executing outbound calls, managing leads, and closing transactions. The brand tone balances executive polish with urgent operational energy: sharp, decisive, luminous, and zero-distraction.

### Design Style & Influences
- **Modern Executive SaaS**: Anchored by clean warm-slate canvas layers (`#F7F7F4`), crisp structural divider lines, and elevated card planes.
- **Kinetic Accentuation**: Strategic use of radiant fiery orange (`#FF6B00`) for prime conversion actions, outbound trigger buttons, and AI-prioritized intelligence markers.
- **Tactile Soft Precision**: Balanced corner rounding (radii scaling up to 1rem - 1.25rem for containers), featherlight inner borders, and micro-diffused ambient elevations.
- **Information Density**: High scannability under pressure; agents glancing between desktop monitors and phone handsets need instant visual hierarchy without cognitive clutter.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-clean, warm-mineral foundation accented by an assertive, energetic warm orange and teal operational cues.

### Roles & Application Rules
- **Primary (`#FF6B00`)**: Reserved strictly for high-intent actions—e.g., initiating calls, AI lead qualification triggers, primary workflow submit buttons, and live audio active-state indicators. Avoid over-saturating data tables with primary orange; use it deliberately to focus attention.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488` / `success-emerald`)**: Serves as the operational pulse—connected call statuses, high AI lead score badges (80+), verified MLS synchronization, and positive margin/commission metrics.
- **Tertiary (`#2563EB`)**: Supporting transactional intelligence—incoming calendar syncs, property contract statuses, and system-level utility links.
- **Neutral (`#14120E`)**: A deep, warm carbon tone that replaces harsh black, providing exceptional contrast for typography and deep interactive states while preserving a bespoke editorial warmth.
- **Surfaces**: Layered hierarchy begins with the warm neutral base (`surface-canvas` `#F7F7F4`), elevates onto immaculate pure card layers (`#FFFFFF`), and utilizes `#F0EFEA` for inactive metric wells, input backings, and table headers.

## Typography

The typography couples the crisp, geometric modernity of **Outfit** for structural headings with the clinical legibility and functional density of **Inter** for dense transactional CRM workflows.

- **Headings (Outfit)**: Used for dashboard metrics, page titles, lead sheet names, and modal banners. Renders numbers and titles with modern authority and high-end agency flair.
- **Body & Controls (Inter)**: Handles call transcripts, lead notes, filter selections, table grids, and input text. Always prioritize strict tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for currency, timestamps, and phone numbers.
- **Uppercase Micro-labels (`label-sm`)**: Form section dividers, badge text, and table column headers must leverage `label-sm` with slight tracking for swift horizontal scanning across CRM records.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an adaptable **12-column responsive fluid grid** with sticky utility sidebars optimized for split-screen calling panels.

### Form Factors & Adaptation
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px screen margin. Features a dual-pane default: 65% width for lead data / pipeline activity, and 35% docked right-hand power dialer / AI copilot console.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout with 16px gutters and 24px screen margin. The calling console collapses into an expandable bottom drawer or slide-over sheet.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 12px gutters and 16px margin. Prioritizes one primary action stack per screen (e.g. Call Card View -> Swipe to next lead).

### Rhythmic Discipline
Interior component spacing relies on dense vertical compactness: `space-sm` (8px) separates interconnected input groups; `space-md` (12px) structures card contents and micro-sections; `space-lg` (20px) pads main dashboard cards.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy skeuomorphic cast shadows, opting instead for **low-contrast borders, tinted ambient diffusions, and tonal tiering**.

### Hierarchy of Surfaces
1. **Canvas (Base Level)**: `#F7F7F4` provides a warm backdrop that reduces optical strain during long dialer sessions.
2. **Cards & Containers (Level 1)**: `#FFFFFF` surfaced with a 1px continuous hairline outline of `border-subtle` (`#E5E4DE`) and a delicate ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(20, 18, 14, 0.03), 0 4px 12px rgba(20, 18, 14, 0.02)`.
3. **Hover & Popovers (Level 2)**: For dropdown menus, quick-view hovercards, and tooltips: 1px border (`#CDCBC2`), elevated with `0 6px 20px rgba(20, 18, 14, 0.06), 0 2px 6px rgba(20, 18, 14, 0.04)`.
4. **Active Calling Drawer / Modals (Level 3)**: Overlay dialer screens and critical contract modals sit on an acrylic backdrop scrim (`rgba(20, 18, 14, 0.35) with 4px backdrop-blur`), casting a deep ambient drop: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(20, 18, 14, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses **Level 2 (Rounded)** standards tailored for sleek corporate agility.

- **Primary Actions & Inputs**: `rounded-md` (6px to 8px) provides clean, confident tactile targets for inputs, table cells, and button controls.
- **Cards & Data Modules**: `rounded-xl` (12px to 16px) establishes friendly, contained modules for property summaries, call histories, and client dossiers.
- **Floating Modals & Calling Docks**: `rounded-2xl` (20px) creates an executive floating apparatus aesthetic.
- **Pills**: Badges, contact tags, and AI lead status pills leverage fully rounded caps (`9999px`) for quick scanning.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Start Call" / Action)**: Background `#FF6B00`, text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight 600. Subtle top inner highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. On hover: `#F26300` with subtle elevation. Active: scale 0.98.
- **Secondary (Data / Filters)**: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E5E4DE`, text `#14120E`. Hover: `#F7F7F4`, border `#CDCBC2`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent background, text `#14120E`, hover: `#F0EFEA`.
- **Dialer Hang-up / Destructive**: Background `#FFF1F2`, text `#E11D48`, hover: `#FFE4E6`, active: border `#E11D48`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Lead Priority Badges**:
  - *Hot AI Lead*: `#FF6B00` 10% tint background, `#FF6B00` text, font-weight 600, paired with an orange pulse indicator dot.
  - *Active Deal / Qualified*: `#ECFDF5` background, `#0D9488` text.
  - *Archived / Stale*: `#F0EFEA` background, `#716F68` text.
- Height: 22px; padding: 2px 8px; border-radius: 9999px; typography: `label-sm`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Search Inputs**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E5E4DE`, corner radius 8px, height 38px. Font-size 14px (`body-md`). Placeholder text `#8E8C85`.
- **Focus State**: Unbroken border `#FF6B00`, accompanied by a 3px soft outer ring in `rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px box with 5px radius. When checked: solid `#FF6B00` with white checkmark icon.

### Cards
- Clean white background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px solid `#E5E4DE` border, 16px radius, padding 20px.
- Card headers feature an inline badge slot on the top right, a 600-weight Outfit headline, and an optional 1px hairline divider separating the body if tabular data follows.

### Domain-Specific Components (Real Estate CRM)
- **Active Call Banner**: Anchored persistent card pinned to the dialer pane. High-contrast lead title, quick-select disposition pills ("Left Voicemail", "Follow Up Scheduled", "Not Interested"), live transcription text feed with highlighted real estate terms (e.g., *budget, pre-approved, timeline*), and a high-visibility, full-width primary button to complete the log.
- **Property Quick-Card**: Split layout containing a 64x64px thumbnail of listing, key metadata (Beds/Baths/Price tabular figures), and single-tap button to transfer details into the live client SMS/Email composer.
